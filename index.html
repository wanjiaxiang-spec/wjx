📄 隐私政策与个人资料说明 / Privacy Policy & Personal Data Notice

1. 我们收集哪些个人资料?

当您在网上下单时,我们仅会收集您主动填写的必要信息:

  • 您的姓名
  • 电话号码(WhatsApp)
  • 取货日期与时间

2. 这些资料用来做什么?

您的资料仅用于:

  • 处理您的订单与核对身份
  • 通过 WhatsApp 与您确认订单详情及通知取货

3. 资料会保存多久?

  • 您在浏览器中暂存的订单记录仅保存在您的手机或电脑本地,您可以随时清除。
  • 为方便您再次选购,您的浏览器会在您自己的手机或电脑上记住您下过单的商品和最近搜索。这些记录不会上传到我们的系统,您可以随时在页面上清除。
  • 为了符合马来西亚税务与商业做账要求,店内的实体销售单据将依法妥善存档,绝不会挪作他用。

4. 资料会被公开或分享给第三方吗?

  • 不会。 我们高度重视您的个人隐私,您的资料仅限本店负责人用于处理订单,绝不用作其他用途。

1. What personal data do we collect?

When you place an order online, we only collect the necessary information that you provide voluntarily:

  • Your name
  • Phone number (WhatsApp)
  • Pickup date and time

2. What do we use your data for?

Your information is used only to:

  • Process your order and verify your identity
  • Confirm your order details and notify you for pickup via WhatsApp

3. How long is your data kept?

  • Order records saved in your browser are stored only on your own phone or computer, and you can clear them at any time.
  • To make re-ordering easier, your browser remembers the items you have ordered and your recent searches on your own phone or computer. These are not uploaded to our system, and you can clear them at any time.
  • To comply with Malaysian tax and business accounting requirements, the shop's physical sales documents are properly filed as required by law and will never be used for any other purpose.

4. Will your data be made public or shared with third parties?

  • No. We take your privacy seriously. Your information is used only by the shop owner to process your order and will never be used for any other purpose.
